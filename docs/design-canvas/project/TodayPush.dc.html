Push copy

Three kinds of notification and nothing else: the morning digest, Ready for a Research now card, and the weekly summary on Sunday. No reminders and no streaks. The phone draws them in its own style; they are drawn here schematically so the copy can be reviewed.

DES-4 · 3 kinds · Web Push

Rules

Digest: at the digest time, only when there is at least one card“Digest: N notes, ~M min”, with “1 note” for one. The body names the first three terms and “and N more”. A tap opens Today and counts as opening the digest.
Ready: once per Research now, when its card is saved“Ready: term”, or “Report ready: term” for an idea. The body gives the category, or for a report the pages read and the reading time. A tap opens the card.
Weekly summary: Sunday, at the digest time“Your week: R read, A added”. The body names the term that came up most and how many notes went to the archive unread. A tap opens the summary.
A private note never shows its term on the lock screenIt reads “a private note” in every notification, and its card opens only inside the app.

Production serves the fonts from the app, never from Google Fonts: Inter and Literata variable woff2, Latin, Latin Extended and Cyrillic subsets, font-display: swap, cached by the service worker. The board loads them from Google Fonts only to draw. Sizes are px at 1x; the reading sizes stay the same at 768 and 1440, where the reading column is capped at 680 px.
